The complete daily routine

Espresso + milk

Bambino Plus · Sculptor 064S
18 g base · manual steam only

Choose a drink. All steps shown below are in brewing order—including the blank preheat shot and cleanup.

Cortado

80–100 ml cup

Espresso-forward, warm milk with barely any foam.

Bean → espresso18 → 36 gDouble · 1:2.0
Cold milk in jug80 gWeigh before steaming
Milk added55 gApprox. finished amount
Pitcher150 mlManual steam
Add air0–1 secJust the initial stretch
Stop milk at55–60°CManual; no auto shutoff
Target textureBarely expanded · silkyMake the smallest glossy microfoam you can; avoid a cappuccino-like cap.

Full workflow

In order · once per drink
1
Power on + preheat. Check water and drip tray. Fit the empty double basket in your bottomless portafilter, lock it in and put the serving cup underneath. Press 2 CUP once for one complete blank double shot; this heats the portafilter and cup.
2
Empty + dry. Discard blank water from the cup; remove the portafilter and wipe the basket completely dry. Put the warm cup on your scale.
3
Grind 18.0 g. Single-dose the Sculptor 064S. Grind into its dosing cup; transfer into the basket with the 54 mm funnel. Keep your dialled-in grind/RPM setting.
4
WDT + tamp. Stir needles lightly from bottom to top; one gentle settling tap; remove funnel; tamp once, firmly and level. Wipe the rim and lock in the portafilter. Puck screen only if you already use one.
5
Pull 36 g espresso. Cup on scale → tare. Hold 2 CUP ≈8 s to pre-infuse → release to extract → press the flashing 2 CUP again to stop near 36 g (allow ~1–2 g of final drips if needed). Start around 27–32 s total from the first button press (first drops ≈8–12 s); adjust the grinder by taste.
6
Steam 80 g cold milk manually. Put milk in the 150 ml pitcher. Raise wand and purge briefly with STEAM on/off. Tip just beneath the milk surface near 3 o’clock; press STEAM. Expose tip slightly to introduce air for 0–1 sec, then submerge slightly and maintain a rolling vortex until 55–60°C. Press STEAM to stop.
7
Wipe, purge + pour. Immediately wipe wand with a damp cloth and lower to auto-purge. Tap jug lightly and swirl until glossy. Swirl the jug and pour ≈55 g smoothly into espresso.
8 · Reset for tomorrow

Knock out puck; rinse the basket/portafilter and dry. Briefly flush the group if coffee residue remains. Empty drip tray as necessary; wipe the worktop.

Optional night-before prep · 30 seconds

Pre-weigh 18.0 g beans, check/refill the Bambino water tank and empty the drip tray. Clean and dry the basket and portafilter. Place WDT, tamper, funnel, scale and cup within reach. Optionally chill your milk/pitcher. The morning routine then begins at step 1.

Shot dial-in · what to change

Begin with 18.0 g in → 36 g out in roughly 27–32 seconds including pre-infusion. Use taste first: sour/thin tends to favour a finer grind (or longer yield); bitter/dry tends to favour a coarser grind (or shorter yield). Change one variable at a time. The 064S has no universal espresso grind number—beans, burr zero and calibration vary. Keep the grinder RPM fixed while dialling in. Use a non-pressurised 54 mm basket sized to hold 18 g.

Milk jugs, quantities & safety

The 150 ml pitcher minimises leftover milk for cortado/mini flat white/Magic/piccolo/macchiato; however, these amounts are small for a powerful wand. Use it only if the tip stays covered, milk can circulate and there is expansion room. If it splashes or cannot form a vortex, use the 480 ml jug and more milk. These are starter quantities, not universal standards; milk aeration changes its volume and accuracy of pouring-by-weight.

Sage officially instructs use of the supplied milk jug and warns against other containers. With a raised wand/manual steaming, the auto temperature cutoff is disabled. Keep hands clear of hot steam; stop STEAM explicitly; wipe the wand and lower it to trigger the automatic purge. A small milk thermometer is optional while developing the feel for 55–65°C.

Why one blank double?

The Bambino Plus can reach ready mode quickly, but a cold metal portafilter and cup absorb heat. The previously established shortcut is one blank 2 CUP run through the locked-in empty basket into the serving cup. Then discard the water and thoroughly dry the basket before grinding. Breville also recommends a short group-head purge to stabilise temperature and clear residue; the single blank preheat combines these jobs.

Equipment used: Bambino Plus, Sculptor 064S, bottomless 54 mm portafilter with non-pressurised double basket, dosing funnel, WDT, level tamper, 150/480 ml pitchers and scale. Also have a clean damp wand cloth. Official Sage SES500 manual.
