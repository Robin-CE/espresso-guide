Methodology

What these numbers mean

Energy return (%) is the measured recovery in the laboratory’s compression test. The forefoot and heel are different measurements. Energy return per gram divides the selected laboratory’s percentage by the same laboratory’s whole-shoe weight.

Percentage points per gram is a custom comparative metric, not energy in joules, a standardized efficiency measurement, or a validated predictor of running economy.

RTINGS and RunRepeat use distinct testing protocols. Their numbers are shown independently. Cross-lab differences are for context only and are marked NOT_HARMONIZED.

Data quality & coverage

The data originates from the audited v1.5 SQLite working copy, not the immutable archival parent. The supplied 260-row RTINGS table is retained separately because per-review dates and revision identifiers were absent. Displayed RTINGS table values are provisionally attributed; underlying reviewed measurements are preserved. RunRepeat-derived mirror values are also provisional. New candidate shoes are unclassified rather than automatically deemed supertrainers.

Foam: Individual-reviewed foam chemistry and RTINGS table-reported primary foam are separate evidence sources. Missing or unknown foam types remain pending, not assumed EVA. Branded names are not proof of polymer chemistry. Reported eligibility is provisional until individual review verification.

Thresholds requiring review: energy return outside 20–95%, weight outside 80–500 g, forefoot–heel difference over 20 pp, cross-lab energy return difference over 15 pp, or weight difference over 30 g/15%. Flags are retained rather than silently correcting source values.

Data and updates

This website is a static, published snapshot. New verified database or table imports require a fresh data.js deployment; edits to the immutable archival parent are prohibited.
